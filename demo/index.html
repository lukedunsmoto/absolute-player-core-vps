AbsolutePlayer

Four video formats your browser can't play properly. With the extension installed, each one plays with sound.

Install: Chrome Web Store or build from source

Compare: flip the toolbar toggle and refresh to see the browser's own playback. The first video loads the FFmpeg engine (~32 MB, cached afterwards).

AC-3 audio

container MKV video H.264 audio AC-3 ✕

Silent without it. The most common broken MKV: DVD and Blu-ray rips. Video is copied untouched, only the audio converted.

DTS audio

container MKV video H.264 audio DTS ✕

Silent without it. The other big "plays, no sound" codec. Same treatment: DTS converted to AAC as it plays.

E-AC-3 5.1 surround

container MKV video H.264 audio E-AC-3 5.1 ✕

Silent without it. "DDP 5.1" in release names. The six-channel track becomes 5.1 AAC, surround preserved.

Xvid-era AVI

container AVI ✕ video MPEG-4 ASP ✕ audio MP3 ✕

Nothing plays without it. The classic 2000s rip: Chromium can't open AVI at all. Both streams are transcoded live.
